How to wear it

Style notes

1Layer heavy over light

Put the heavier piece — the hoodie or jacket — over a plain, lighter tee. It keeps the fit from feeling bulky no matter the size.

2Match one loud piece

Pick one item to stand out — the cap, the sneakers, or the jacket — and keep the rest of the outfit in solid, quiet colours.

3Let denim break naturally

A slight break at the ankle over the sneaker looks better than a hard crease — go one size true rather than long.
